Manaslu is what the Annapurna Circuit was thirty years ago: a full circuit of an 8,000m mountain, teahouse to teahouse, with a real high pass — and a fraction of the foot traffic. It sits in a restricted area, which means the paperwork is stricter and a licensed guide isn't just sensible here, it's the law. That keeps the crowds away and the trail honest.

## How long it takes

Fourteen days is typical: drive from Kathmandu to Machha Khola, walk up the wild Budhi Gandaki gorge for a week, acclimatise at Samagaon (3,530m) under Manaslu's enormous east face, then cross the Larkya La (5,106m) — the crux of the trek — and descend into the Annapurna region to finish.

## How hard it is

Strenuous. The gorge days are long, the teahouses simpler than Khumbu, and the Larkya La is a serious pass: a pre-dawn start, hours above 4,500m, and a long, steep descent, often on snow. You should have hill fitness and ideally one previous trek at altitude. This is the step up after Everest Base Camp or the Annapurna Circuit — not instead of them.

## The best time to go

Autumn (September–November) is prime — stable air for the pass crossing. Spring (March–May) works too, with more snow lingering on the Larkya La. The pass can close in bad weather in any season; build in a spare day.

## The restricted-area permit — read this

Manaslu requires a Restricted Area Permit, issued only through a registered agency, with a minimum group of two trekkers plus a licensed guide. Solo trekking is not permitted here at all. We handle the paperwork — your guide's licence and our agency registration are exactly what the checkpoints want to see — but the permit needs lead time, so book earlier than you would for Everest Base Camp.

Q: Why does the Manaslu Circuit require a guide?
A: It crosses a government-designated restricted area near the Tibetan border. The rules require a registered agency, a licensed guide, and at least two trekkers. Checkpoints do check.

Q: Is the Manaslu Circuit harder than Everest Base Camp?
A: Yes. Similar maximum altitude, but a genuine pass crossing, longer walking days, and simpler lodges. It rewards previous altitude experience.

Q: When should I book a Manaslu trek?
A: At least 4–6 weeks out. The restricted-area permit paperwork needs your passport details and lead time — later than that gets tight.
